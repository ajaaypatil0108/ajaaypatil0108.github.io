Chipotle Mexican Grill (CMG): Integrated Three-Statement FP&A Model & Dashboard

Jun 2026 to present | Self-directed portfolio project

A driver-based income statement, balance sheet and cash flow model built by hand from Chipotle’s FY2025 Form 10-K. It forecasts FY2026–2027, compares FY2025 actuals to a budget built from management’s guidance, and flexes the whole model with one Base / Bull / Bear switch.

The variance bridge shows the FY2025 revenue miss came almost entirely from lower sales per restaurant, mainly comparable sales of −1.7% vs. +4.0% budgeted, not from openings (334 vs. 330). Findings are packaged in a Power BI dashboard with 8 DAX measures and a written FP&A memo.

DFW Rental Underwriting Model: Single-Family Rental Screen & 10-Year Pro Forma

Jul 2026 to Aug 2026 | Self-directed portfolio project

An end-to-end underwriting model in Excel and Power Query that asks whether buying a single-family rental in Dallas–Fort Worth earns a return at today’s prices and a 7% mortgage. It ranks 166 DFW ZIP codes on gross rental yield (70%) and 3-year rent growth (30%), skips the top three as possible yield traps, and underwrites 76110 Fort Worth over a 10-year hold.

The verdict is a disciplined pass: negative leverage pulls the levered IRR below the unlevered return, DSCR stays under 1.0 for all ten years, and cash flow is negative every year.
